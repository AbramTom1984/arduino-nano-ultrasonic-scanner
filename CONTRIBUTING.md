# Contributing

Improvements, documentation fixes, and build reports are welcome.

Before proposing a change, describe what it changes, why it is useful, and how it was checked. For hardware changes include the board, supply arrangement, wiring, and photographs where helpful. Do not claim hardware testing unless performed.

Keep scan parameters consistent between the Arduino and Processing programs. Preserve the copyright and license notices. Contributions should be compatible with the MIT license and should not include credentials or material you do not have permission to share.
